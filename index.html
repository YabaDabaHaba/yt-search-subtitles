Subtitles, in VRChat

Subtitles and translations for YouTube and Bilibili videos in VRChat worlds with YT Search. Everyone in the instance sees them.

Download for Windows
Free · one .exe, nothing to install Fetching the latest release…

Windows may warn you the first time: the app isn't signed, which costs money every year. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Three steps

1

Run it

It opens over VRChat's window. Ctrl+Shift+Y shows or hides it, even in game. It keeps running in the tray.

2

Allow Untrusted URLs

In VRChat's settings. The world asks the app on your own PC for subtitles, and VRChat only allows that with this on.

3

Play a video

In a world with YT Search. Subtitles appear under the screen a few seconds later. One person running the app is enough.

What it does

A translation line

The spoken line, and under it the language you pick: YouTube's own subtitles when the video has them, its machine translation when not.

今日はいい天気ですねThe weather is nice today, isn't it?

Bilibili too

Log in by scanning a QR code with the Bilibili app. The login stays on your PC and is sent only to Bilibili.

Out of your way

A small status line over VRChat when something changes, clicks pass through it. It only reads VRChat's log, so your game doesn't slow down.

Windows 10 or 11. Desktop VRChat in windowed or borderless mode shows the overlay; in VR the subtitles still work, the overlay just stays on the desktop. The app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, already part of current Windows; without it, it opens as a plain window.

Subtitles come from YouTube and Bilibili. The app needs no account, except a Bilibili login for Bilibili's subtitles.
